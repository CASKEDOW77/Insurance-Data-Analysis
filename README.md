# Insurance-Data-Analysis
## Project Objective
This project focuses on insurance data analysis using Power BI. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of policy performance, customer demographics, claim trends, and premium distributions. The aim is to assist stakeholders in making informed decisions by visualizing complex datasets and providing actionable insights.
## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/CASKEDOW77/Insurance-Data-Analysis/blob/main/InsuranceData.csv">Dataset</a>
## Questions (KPIs)
- Which policy types generate the highest claims, and how can risk management be optimized?
- How does claim rejection vary across policy types, and what corrective measures can reduce rejections?
- Which customer age group has the most inactive policies, and how can retention rates be improved?
- What proportion of pending claims belong to specific policy types, and how can their processing be expedited?
- Are there specific demographic segments (e.g., gender or age group) with higher claim settlement success rates?
- How does the premium-to-claim ratio differ by policy type, and which products are most profitable?
- Can inactive customers be reactivated with targeted campaigns, and what factors contribute to inactivity?
- What is the correlation between policy type and coverage amount, and how can coverage be optimized for customer needs?

## Project Insight
- Policy Type Performance: Uncovered the most and least popular policy types based on premium amounts, aiding targeted promotions.
- Age Group Analysis: Revealed that adults contribute the highest claim amounts, while young adults have the lowest claims, indicating differing risk profiles.
- Gender Distribution: Equal gender distribution in policies suggests balanced customer acquisition strategies but opens room for gender-specific product customization.
- Claim Rejection Patterns: Identified potential areas of policy misalignment or customer dissatisfaction leading to high claim rejection rates.
- Customer Segmentation: Segmented customers based on active/inactive policies to design reactivation campaigns for inactive users.
- Pending Claims Impact: Highlighted the financial impact of pending claims on business cash flow and operational inefficiencies.
- Cross-Selling Opportunities: Insights into multi-policy customers, enabling strategies for cross-selling or up-selling complementary policies.
